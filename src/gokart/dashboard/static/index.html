Fault recovery

  1. Fix the cause (release both pedals if needed, wait for readings to settle).
  2. Click Clear fault — for critical faults this power-cycles the system; you may need to wait for pack voltage to drop first.
  3. Click Arm with brake and drive again.

Or use New session to reset everything without refreshing the page.
